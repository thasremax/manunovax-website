PROJECT OVERVIEW

Making everyday communication easier across languages.

ManuNovaX is developing a senior-focused, two-way voice translation concept for retirement homes, seniors living independently and multilingual family environments. The goal is to make communication more natural and comfortable for residents while helping caregivers and family members understand and respond.

01

Resident-centred communication

The concept is designed around each resident’s preferred language and communication preferences, with a simple experience that can reduce the effort required to communicate across language barriers.

02

Wearable and QR-enabled access

A dedicated wearable or resident device may connect with a mobile application. A caregiver could use a QR code to access the appropriate resident communication profile, subject to authorization and privacy controls.

03

A familiar listening experience

The development direction includes configurable spoken translation and voice preferences, with the aim of making translated communication easier and more reassuring for the resident.

WHO IT MAY SERVE

Potential application areas

Retirement homes, senior-care providers, caregivers, families and organizations supporting multilingual communities.

CURRENT DEVELOPMENT FOCUS

Accessible two-way voice translation, resident-centred design, authorized access and privacy-conscious workflows.

This innovation is under development. Features, performance and implementation pathways remain subject to research, engineering validation, privacy and security assessment, and any applicable regulatory requirements.
