ABOUT MANUNOVAX

Engineering Expertise. Artificial Intelligence. Real Manufacturing Results.

ManuNovaX Technologies Inc. combines manufacturing engineering, artificial intelligence, automation, and digital engineering tools to help manufacturers solve practical production challenges.

We work across the manufacturing lifecycle — from product and process development to production improvement, automation, simulation, costing, quotation, and technical documentation. Our focus is not simply on recommending technology; it is on developing solutions that can be implemented on the shop floor and measured through real business results.

By combining engineering knowledge with AI-driven methods, ManuNovaX helps manufacturers identify opportunities faster, make better technical and commercial decisions, reduce avoidable costs, and build more efficient and scalable operations.
