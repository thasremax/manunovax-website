PROJECT OVERVIEW

Exploring a new approach to mobile traffic monitoring.

ManuNovaX is developing an intelligent, vehicle-based traffic monitoring concept that combines imaging, data processing and a connected reporting workflow. The project explores how verified participating vehicles could support the identification and review of potential traffic violations, with an emphasis on timely information and accountable validation.

01

Mobile monitoring concept

The system is being designed around cameras installed in approved participating vehicles, rather than relying only on conventional fixed roadside installations.

02

Central review and validation

Captured events would be transmitted to a secure central workflow for assessment. The concept distinguishes events suitable for automated processing from events that require human review.

03

Timely, accountable communication

The proposed workflow explores faster notifications to relevant parties while preserving validation steps, privacy safeguards and appropriate oversight.

WHO IT MAY SERVE

Potential application areas

Transportation authorities, municipalities, road-safety organizations, fleet operators and technology partners.

CURRENT DEVELOPMENT FOCUS

Mobile imaging, reliable event detection, validation workflows, secure data handling and regulatory alignment.

This innovation is under development. Features, performance and implementation pathways remain subject to research, engineering validation, privacy and security assessment, and any applicable regulatory requirements.
