PROJECT OVERVIEW

Building on existing cameras to support earlier awareness.

ManuNovaX is developing a home-security innovation intended to complement existing camera and DVR installations. The concept focuses on identifying potentially suspicious activity and delivering relevant alerts, with an optional review process and carefully considered community notification features.

01

Designed to complement existing cameras

The development direction is to connect with compatible existing security-camera and DVR systems, helping homeowners explore added intelligence without necessarily replacing all installed equipment.

02

Context-aware activity alerts

The concept aims to identify activity that may warrant attention and send relevant notifications, rather than overwhelming users with unnecessary routine or system-status messages.

03

Optional review and community awareness

A central review option and controlled notifications to participating nearby residents are being explored, with privacy, consent and safeguards treated as important design requirements.

WHO IT MAY SERVE

Potential application areas

Homeowners, residential communities, property managers, security integrators and technology partners.

CURRENT DEVELOPMENT FOCUS

Compatibility with existing video systems, meaningful alerts, privacy-conscious sharing and human review options.

This innovation is under development. Features, performance and implementation pathways remain subject to research, engineering validation, privacy and security assessment, and any applicable regulatory requirements.
