PROJECT OVERVIEW

Adding an identity-aware layer to vehicle security.

ManuNovaX is developing an intelligent vehicle anti-theft concept that uses in-vehicle imaging and a trusted-person authorization workflow. The goal is to help vehicle owners respond when an unfamiliar person attempts to use a vehicle, while keeping registered people informed and providing a structured way to approve or prevent access.

01

Driver identity awareness

The concept explores identifying whether the person in the driver’s seat is recognized as an authorized user, using an in-vehicle camera and appropriate identity safeguards.

02

Trusted-person notifications and authorization

Registered contacts may receive alerts when an unfamiliar driver is detected. The proposed workflow includes designated approval and the ability for registered users to raise concerns.

03

Vehicle-use protection and special modes

The development work considers how the authorization process should operate before vehicle use, including appropriate service or maintenance situations and clear owner controls.

WHO IT MAY SERVE

Potential application areas

Vehicle owners, automotive security providers, fleet operators, insurers and automotive technology partners.

CURRENT DEVELOPMENT FOCUS

Driver verification, trusted-contact workflows, secure notifications, owner control and responsible handling of identity data.

This innovation is under development. Features, performance and implementation pathways remain subject to research, engineering validation, privacy and security assessment, and any applicable regulatory requirements.
